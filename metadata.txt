about=Aino connects natural-language geospatial data search and multistep analysis with your QGIS workflow.
    Start with a text request in an empty or existing QGIS project. Aino resolves the search area from your request and asks for clarification when needed. You can also use the current map view, selected polygons, or a point and radius, and include explicitly selected vector layers with their current edits.
    GO prepares the inputs, links the project to Aino and starts the analysis. Follow progress, answer questions, review plans and read the report in a dockable panel. After completion, successful vector results are automatically imported as persistent, editable GeoPackage layers with supported styling. Open the linked project in Aino to continue in the web application.
    Interrupted operations can be resumed, and saved results can be imported again without starting a new analysis. Save the QGIS project explicitly to retain its Aino link and layers.
    An Aino account and an internet connection are required. Service usage and access to data sources depend on your account. GDAL 3.7 or newer is required.
